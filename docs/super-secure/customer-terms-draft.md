# Super Secure customer terms — draft

**For owner and legal review only. Not approved or effective terms.** These terms do not authorize customer data collection or override the published privacy policy, a signed DPA, or applicable law. Replace bracketed items and have counsel review before launch.

## 1. Service and eligibility

Super Secure is an invite-only organization text-messaging feature. Access is available only in a build approved by Dost and to accounts that satisfy the app's adult eligibility checks. The organization owner is responsible for selecting authorized members and sharing the organization ID privately. The owner and managers admit members by Firebase UID. Members may not invite or add people.

An organization may have no more than 25 active members, including its owner and managers. The MVP provides one group conversation and direct text conversations between current members. Messages are limited to 4,000 characters. Media, audio/video calling, public discovery, and member-created groups are not supported.

## 2. Roles and organization responsibility

The owner is the organization's administrator and billing contact, if a separately approved plan is later offered. Managers can add or remove members; only the owner can appoint or remove managers. At least the owner remains a manager. The organization is responsible for lawful authority to provide member identifiers and display names to Dost and for its members' appropriate use of the service.

Removing a member blocks future server-authorized access but does not erase historical messages. Organization data is retained until Dost verifies an owner deletion request. Owners can contact **tushartk018@gmail.com** for deletion or to discuss an export/migration request.

## 3. Security and service limitations

Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest. Super Secure is **not end-to-end encrypted**: Firebase administrators/provider personnel with privileged access may technically access stored data. Group messages are available to current organization members; direct messages are limited to their two current participants through the application.

The MVP uses Dost's existing Firebase project and region; it is not hosted on a customer-managed server. Automated data export, migration, and self-service organization deletion are not available. Do not rely on an export or migration until Dost confirms the scope, schedule, and secure delivery in writing.

## 4. Data use and third-party processing

Dost processes organization data to provide membership, audit, and text-messaging functions. Dost does not use this data for model training, product improvement, testing, experiments, analytics, advertising, or AI services. Firebase/Firestore provides hosting and message delivery. Organization data is governed by the published Super Secure privacy disclosures and, where signed, the parties' DPA.

## 5. Acceptable use and availability

Customers and members must not use the service for unlawful activity, threaten or harass others, or submit data they lack authority to provide. Dost may restrict access when necessary to protect users, comply with law, or address a security incident. The MVP has no end-to-end encryption, guaranteed availability, or emergency-response service.

These draft terms require jurisdiction, governing-law, liability, dispute, support, and service-level provisions to be selected by the owner and counsel. No placeholder in this document is a legal commitment. Contact **tushartk018@gmail.com** with questions.
